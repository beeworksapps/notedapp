A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you
make a note.

Does Noted read my entire inbox?

No. On iPhone, you configure a Shortcuts automation. Noted receives the message content you pass to it; it does not have general access to your SMS inbox or notification history.

Do I need to install ChatGPT?

No. Noted’s AI processing uses its own backend connection. An internet connection and the appropriate account and plan access are required.

What if a transaction isn’t recognized?

Missing or uncertain details can send it to Message review. Check the account, amount, currency, category and date before recording it.

Can I use more than one currency?

Yes. Each account keeps its currency. Combined current totals use approximate exchange rates when available; these are not guaranteed bank conversion rates.

What are the subscription options?

Noted is the monthly essentials plan. Noted Pro is the annual plan with expanded features and AI allowances. Final localized prices, limits and trial eligibility are shown in the app before purchase.
